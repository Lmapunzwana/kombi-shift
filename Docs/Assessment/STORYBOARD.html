Kombi Shift 2 storyboard

Current final-design storyboard, 9 November 2026 expansion. This is not backdated early-user research.

1 Title

Choose stage; see minutes and passenger target.

2 Learn

Read controls; boarding requires stopping below 2 km/h.

3 Approach

Minimap links the player, active station and rivals.

4 Board

Reserve queue; walk to separate seats; assign downstream destinations. Fare waits for delivery.

5 Deliver

Hear the stop bell; stop at the requested intermediate station; animate alighting, earn fare and XP. Finish at Mbare.

6 Result

Wait for delivered passengers; show success/failure and replay.

UI flow

Title → Instructions / Settings → Title → Morning or Rush Hour → Gameplay → Results → Replay / Menu.
Gameplay → Pause → Resume / Restart / Menu.
Morning success → Rush Hour.

Communication

Menu → Challenge → Director → Station → Passenger → Fare callback → Director → HUD / Result

The director owns rules and time. Stations own reservations. Passengers own movement and pose. Rivals use the same queue service. RCC handles vehicle physics.

Expanded service and consequence flows

7 Stop request

A passenger approaches their destination. A bell and text identify the request; the minimap highlights it. Annotation: the driver has time to brake.

8 Miss and recover

Driving past a requested stop reduces that passenger’s happiness and driver XP once. A nearby serviced station can recover the trip. Annotation: the passenger is not trapped.

9 Competition

A coloured rival approaches, reserves a queue and physically boards people. Its total rises as the shared queue empties. Annotation: the player sees opportunity disappear.

10 Road law

Speed and stop controls have zone triggers. Persistent overspeed receives one game fine after grace. Annotation: the HUD explains the incident and separate fine total.

11 Condition

A meaningful collision reduces condition and bounded engine/braking performance. Stop at a service point and pay T repair. Annotation: repair requires earnings and cannot happen while moving.

12 Hump

A warning precedes a shallow physical ramp. Slow for suspension comfort; severe overspeed causes a complaint and condition loss. Annotation: actual RCC physics is tested separately from geometry.
