# Open, play and understand Kombi Shift

Use Unity 6000.6.2f1. `UnityStarter` is the Built-In desktop/WebGL project. `UnityHDRP` is the separate HDRP desktop project. Never copy one project's ProjectSettings over the other. No upgrade of Leroy's original foundation is implied.

1. Run `Open_Kombi_Unity.sh` (HDRP) or `Open_Kombi_Unity.sh builtin`. Alternatively add the appropriate directory in Unity Hub and open it.
2. Open `Assets/KombiShiftJayden/Generated/KombiShiftStarter.unity`.
3. Open **Kombi Shift > Open Game Workbench**, then press Play. A small startup root is intentional: the environment is generated by C# when Play begins. Expand the root to inspect vehicles, stops, passenger pools and managers.
4. Start CBD. W/S accelerate/reverse, A/D steer, Space brakes and latches hold, E boards/drops/buys fuel, H horns, R recovers for $1, Escape pauses. Click the Game view for keyboard focus. Mouse drive buttons and optional touch controls are available.
5. Stop inside Market Square's painted bay and board. The hwindi walks to the sliding door and helps passengers. Read individual destinations and patience on the left. Click map stops to choose a destination. Drive there, stop and press E. Fare and cash rise after alighting.
6. Visit the fuel station; E buys fuel only if you can afford it. The nearby REPAIR $3 button restores 30 health and 35 brake/suspension condition. Fuel and repairs debit cash; remittance and the optional conductor are settled in the shift statement.
7. Repeat on the rainy highway. Compare grip, fuel, route length, hazards and rival aggression. Reach the target to win; empty fuel, zero health or time below target loses. Restart and return to menu work from the pause/statement screens.

## Explore safely using the workbench

In Play, the workbench can start either stage, park at a numbered stop, service it, damage the vehicle or restore test resources. It displays actual passenger count, cash, fares, fuel, health and rival state. These tools are confined to the Unity Editor; they are not release cheats.

Select `Assets/KombiShiftJayden/Generated/GameTuning.asset` before Play to change acceleration, steering, stage targets, duration, starting fuel, traffic, patience or aggression. Stop Play before saving changes: modifications to generated scene objects during Play normally disappear. The tuning asset remains the persistent source of balance values.

The Catalog folder contains reusable Kombi, Passenger, Road Straight 10m, Rank shelter and Fuel pump prefabs. Drop them into a separate art scene for editing and presentation; the procedural gameplay generator remains the source of the built world. Editing a catalog prefab does not automatically change the generator.
