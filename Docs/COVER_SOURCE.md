# Title-screen source

The current title screen uses a live Unity 3D scene, not a background illustration. Hiace Showcase is a stripped presentation copy of the current player vehicle. Its existing model and materials retain their original licence requirements. The rank/workshop stage, camera movement, interface, animation and sound feedback were made locally.

Barlow Condensed is supplied under SIL OFL 1.1; the licence is included in Assets/KombiShift2/Resources/Fonts. Online design references are recorded in MENU_DESIGN.md. No screenshots or artwork from those games were imported.

The historical hiace-gameplay-cover.png and its old generation tool are preserved outside the Unity project in ../_Archive_Structure_Cleanup; they are no longer loaded by the current menu. The earlier generated cover is outside Assets in the local backup. Cast and received shadows remain disabled.
